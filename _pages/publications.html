DAGFormer: Global Topology Prediction for Transformer Cross-Layer Connections

Xiaocong Yang*, Yuren Hao*, ChengXiang Zhai · *Equal contribution

A small causal Transformer reads the input tokens and plans every cross-layer connection of the backbone at once: what each attention head's query, key and value, and each layer's residual input, reads from the embedding and all earlier layers.

  • On matched OLMo-2 pairs from 75M to 1B parameters, DAGFormer lowers WikiText-2 perplexity by 17% to 25%. A 1B DAGFormer trained on 5B tokens beats the vanilla 1B trained on 10B.
  • Lowest perplexity of four routing baselines at 75M, 300M and 1B. The gain carries over to 13 evaluation tasks (+2.0 to +2.5 mean accuracy), to instruction finetuning (63% to 70% AlpacaEval win rate) and to structured pruning.
  • The topology is planned once, from the tokens alone, so the whole graph can be read as one object.

Sparse Autoencoders Are a Shared Coordinate System for Symbolic Matching

Xiaocong Yang, Xuanyu Meng, Yuji Zhang, Jiawei Han, ChengXiang Zhai

SAE-IDF matches symbols across proof assistants, programming languages, table schemas, knowledge graphs and ontologies. It reads each symbol with an LLM, encodes it with a pretrained sparse autoencoder and weights each feature by its rarity. Nothing is trained.

  • On 12 tasks across 6 kinds of symbolic system, SAE-IDF beats the same model's dense hidden state on every task with Gemma-2-9B and on all but one with Llama-3.1-8B, by up to 0.45 MRR (Lean 4 → Metamath).
  • Needs no training and no aligned pairs: the pretrained SAE serves as a coordinate system that different symbolic systems share.
  • Every coordinate is a named feature, so each match splits into a few rare concepts. Without the weighting, features active on more than 1% of the Pile carry 56% to 91% of the cosine; with it, 0% to 6%.

NeurIPS 2024

Cascade Speculative Drafting for Even Faster LLM Inference

Ziyi Chen, Xiaocong Yang, Jiacheng Lin, Chenkai Sun, Kevin Chen-Chuan Chang, Jie Huang

Speculative decoding lets a small draft model propose tokens that a large target model verifies in parallel. Cascade Speculative Drafting speeds up the drafting itself, by cascading draft models in two directions.

Paper
  • The cascade is proved to be more efficient than standard speculative decoding and reaches up to 81% additional speedup in experiments.
  • As in standard speculative decoding, the target model verifies every drafted token, so the output distribution is the target model's own.
